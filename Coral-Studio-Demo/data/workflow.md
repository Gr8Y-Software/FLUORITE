## A good process should feel simple.

### 01 · Start with the idea
Create a project or open one you already have. Bring your content and assets into a workspace that is ready to use.

### 02 · Shape the experience
Edit your pages and styles, arrange the content, and keep site settings close by. Use Markdown for writing and code for the details.

### 03 · Build and review
Build the site with a single action. Open the local preview, check the layout at different widths, and make the small refinements that make a page feel finished.

### 04 · Publish your way
The output is a static site you can host with the service that suits your project. Your source remains a familiar directory of files.

> Stay focused on the work. Let the workspace keep the pieces together.
